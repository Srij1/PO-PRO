## PO PRO: DEBATE 
PO PRO is an app dedicated to making running congressional debate rounds simple and easy. It tracks speaker order, aff and neg speeches, questioning periods, legislation progress, and voting results. It was created to make the work of the PO simple and streamlined so even novices can fill the role. It was created by Srijan Tripathi, an 11th grader at Elkhorn South High School.

## Features

- **Chamber Management**
  - Add competitors and automatically randomizes speaker precedence
  - Track speeches and questioning history
  - Constantly updates chamber order and precedence

- **Debate Workflow**
  - Guides the PO through each stage of debate
  - Manages speeches, questioning, voting, and bill transitions
  - Automatically alternate affirmative and negative speeches
  - Supports ammendment speeches
  - Supports second sessions with the same chamber (reverse precedence)

- **Timers**
  - Built-in speech timers
  - 3 min 10 second speeches, 120/60 second questioning periods based on speech number

- **Legislation Management**
  - Import docket
  - Tracks the current bill being debated
  - Moves between bills

- **Voting & Records**
  - Record votes for bills and ammendemnts
  - Maintains full  debate history
  - Can export debate history in .csv format

# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 18, 2026

PO Pro ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## Information Collection

PO Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

The app allows users to enter information such as:

- Competitor names
- Legislation titles
- Debate notes
- Voting records
- Chamber information

This information is stored locally on the user's device and is only used for app functionality.

## Data Storage

All user-created content is stored locally within the application. PO Pro does not upload, sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties.

If the application is deleted, locally stored information may also be removed depending on device settings.

## Third-Party Services

PO Pro does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or tracking technologies.

## Children's Privacy

PO Pro is designed for student use, including middle school and high school speech and debate programs. The app does not  collect personal information from children or any users.

## Data Security

Because PO Pro does not collect or transmit user data, there is no external database containing user information.
