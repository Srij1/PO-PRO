## PO Pro 
PO PRO is an app dedicated to making running congressional debate rounds simple and easy. It tracks speaker order, aff and neg speeches, questioning periods, legislation progress, and voting results. PO Pro reduces the POs workload so they can focus on running a fair and efficient chamber.

## Features

- **Chamber Management**
  - Add competitors and automatically organize speaker precedence
  - Track speeches and questioning history
  - Maintain an updated chamber order

- **Debate Workflow**
  - Guide the PO through each stage of debate
  - Manage speeches, questioning, voting, and bill transitions
  - Automatically alternate affirmative and negative speeches

- **Timers**
  - Built-in speech timers
  - Support for Congressional Debate speech and questioning formats
  - Automatic timing based on speech type

- **Legislation Management**
  - Import and organize a docket
  - Track the current bill being debated
  - Move between pieces of legislation seamlessly

- **Voting & Records**
  - Record chamber votes
  - Maintain debate history
  - Provide a digital replacement for traditional PO sheets

## Purpose

PO Pro was created to make Congressional Debate more accessible, efficient, and organized. By digitizing the tools used by Presiding Officers, the app allows students to spend less time managing paperwork and more time focusing on quality debate.

## Technology

PO Pro is built using:

- Swift
- SwiftUI
- Xcode

# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 18, 2026

PO Pro ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## Information Collection

PO Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

The app allows users to enter information such as:

- Competitor names
- Legislation titles
- Debate notes
- Voting records
- Chamber information

This information is stored locally on the user's device and is only used for app functionality.

## Data Storage

All user-created content is stored locally within the application. PO Pro does not upload, sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties.

If the application is deleted, locally stored information may also be removed depending on device settings.

## Third-Party Services

PO Pro does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or tracking technologies.

## Children's Privacy

PO Pro is designed for student use, including middle school and high school speech and debate programs. The app does not  collect personal information from children or any users.

## Data Security

Because PO Pro does not collect or transmit user data, there is no external database containing user information. \
